# Authorship and Use of AI Assistance

This project was designed, structured, implemented, and documented by Sara Hebbadj as part of the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme at the University of Hull.

The overall project idea, system architecture, recommendation logic, application flow, and final written explanations are the author’s own work.

Limited AI-assisted tools, including code-completion support, were used in some parts of development for minor boilerplate generation, syntax suggestions, and routine implementation support. These tools were not used to independently define the project contribution, recommendation design, evaluation, or dissertation argument.

All final decisions regarding project structure, integration, debugging, testing, documentation, and submission were made by the author.
